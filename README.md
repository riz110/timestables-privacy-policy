# Privacy Policy for Times Tables Master

**Last Updated:** February 14, 2026

## Overview
Times Tables Master ("the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This app does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

## Data Collection
**We collect NO data.** Specifically:
- No personal information
- No usage analytics
- No advertising IDs
- No location data
- No contact information
- No network requests to external servers

## Data Storage
All app data is stored **locally on your device only**:
- User profiles (names, progress, coins)
- Quiz and practice statistics
- Master PIN and child PINs (stored as SHA-256 hashed values, never in plain text)
- Session history and achievements

**This data never leaves your device.**

## Device Authentication
The app uses Android's built-in biometric authentication (fingerprint, face unlock, or device PIN) for master PIN recovery. This authentication happens entirely on your device through Android's secure APIs. No biometric data is transmitted, stored, or accessed by the app.

## Third-Party Services
The app does NOT use:
- Analytics services (Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, etc.)
- Advertising networks
- Social media integrations
- Cloud storage or backup services
- Push notification services
- In-app purchase tracking

## Children's Privacy
This app is designed for children to practice multiplication and division. We take children's privacy seriously:
- No data collection of any kind
- No internet connectivity required
- All data stays on the device
- Parental controls via PIN protection
- No external links or web browser access

We do not knowingly collect any information from children under 13 years of age.

## Data Security
While we don't collect data, we take security seriously:
- PINs are hashed using SHA-256 encryption
- All data is stored using Android's encrypted DataStore
- Device-level authentication for sensitive operations
- No network transmission of any kind

## Data Retention
All data remains on your device until:
- You uninstall the app, OR
- You perform a factory reset within the app, OR
- You clear the app's data through Android settings

There are no backups to external servers, so data cannot be recovered if deleted.

## Permissions
This app requires NO special permissions:
- No camera access
- No microphone access
- No location access
- No contacts access
- No file storage access (except app-specific storage)

The only implicit permission is USE_BIOMETRIC for optional fingerprint/face unlock, which is handled entirely by Android's secure system.

## Changes to This Policy
We may update this privacy policy from time to time to reflect changes in the app or legal requirements. Changes will be reflected in app updates with a new "Last Updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

## Your Rights
Since we don't collect any data, there is no data to:
- Request access to
- Request deletion of
- Request correction of
- Request transfer of

All your data is already under your complete control on your device.
